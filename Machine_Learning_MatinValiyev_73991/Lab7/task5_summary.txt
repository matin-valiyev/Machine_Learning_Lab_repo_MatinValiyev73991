Lab 7 Task 5: Efficiency Pillars Summary

MODEL              | DATA                           | MODEL SIZE                   | COMPUTE (Latency) | ENERGY
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Baseline CNN       | Fashion-MNIST labels           | ~1.01M params (3.88 MB)      | ~0.86 ms          | High
Pruned CNN (90%)   | Fashion-MNIST labels           | ~1.01M params (3.70 MB zip)  | ~1.46 ms          | High
Plain Student      | Fashion-MNIST labels           | ~105k params (~0.4 MB)       | Fast (~0.2 ms)    | Low
Distilled Student  | Fashion-MNIST + Teacher Logits | ~105k params (~0.4 MB)       | Fast (~0.2 ms)    | Low

Conclusion:
Unstructured magnitude pruning successfully reduced the theoretical storage size of the baseline model when compressed,
but it negatively impacted compute latency on a standard CPU because dense kernels still process the zeroed weights.
Knowledge distillation proved to be the most effective optimization technique.
By transferring the teacher model's soft probabilities to the student, 
the distilled model achieved near-baseline accuracy while reducing the model size by roughly 90% and significantly improving compute latency and energy efficiency.
